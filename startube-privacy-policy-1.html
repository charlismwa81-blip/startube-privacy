Startube Privacy Policy

Effective date: 9 November 2026

1. Introduction

Startube is a music application that allows users to play music stored on their devices and access online music. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your information.

2. Information We Collect

Startube does not require users to create an account or provide personal information to use its main features. Third-party advertising services may collect device identifiers, advertising data and app usage information.

3. Device Permissions

Startube may request access to music and audio files stored on your device. This permission is used to display and play your local music.

4. Online Music

When accessing online music, your device connects to the relevant online services. Those services may process information according to their own privacy policies.

5. Advertising

Startube uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. Google AdMob may collect and process information such as device identifiers, IP address, advertising interactions and usage information to provide and measure advertisements, and to personalize advertisements where permitted by applicable settings and law.

For more information, visit Google's Privacy Policy.

6. Data Sharing

Startube does not sell users' personal information. Information may be processed by third-party services such as Google AdMob as necessary to provide advertising and related services.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information associated with the application.

8. Children's Privacy

Startube is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when necessary. Any changes will be published on this page.
